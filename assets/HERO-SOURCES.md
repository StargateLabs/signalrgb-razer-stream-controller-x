# Hero artwork provenance

Backgrounds are reused from the owner’s Strata Mod project (`performance-lab-photo.webp`, `benchmark-lab-photo.webp`, `monitor-lab-photo.webp`) at the owner’s explicit request. The original Stargate Labs logo is embedded separately. SVG overlays provide text, contrast and rounded framing; the source raster files are not modified. These images are brand artwork and laboratory imagery, not new device benchmarks or evidence of performance. Third-party marks visible within the original images belong to their owners.
